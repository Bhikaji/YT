## 1. Compliance Standard & Legal Principles

To maintain 100% eligibility for commercial monetization, ad revenue sharing, and copyright protection on YouTube:
1. **Zero Copyright Infringement:** No copyrighted third-party characters, unlicensed music, or protected audio tracks.
2. **Original Creative Contribution:** The automated 3D pipeline utilizes original procedurally-assembled geometry, original scripted dialogue, and unique voice profiles, avoiding the "Reused Content" penalty.
3. **Copyleft Boundary Enforcement:** Output media (MP4 videos, thumbnails, subtitle tracks) generated by open-source tools (GPL/LGPL software like Blender, MPFB2, FFmpeg, and Piper) are considered **user-created creative output** under copyright law and do **not** inherit copyleft obligations.

## 3. Prohibited & Blacklisted Asset Classes

The following are strictly banned from the autonomous production queue:
- **Commercial Voice Clones of Living Celebrities**: Prohibited by YouTube Community Guidelines & state likeness laws. All character voice profiles are synthetic or trained on open-source datasets.
- **Academic-Only Datasets (e.g. Human3.6M, SMPL commercial lock)**: Prohibited in the production line.
- **Copyrighted Ambient Music Tracks**: Prohibited. Audio stems must be strictly original or CC0.
